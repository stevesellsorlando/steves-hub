## About
- **Hub name:** Steve's Hub
- **Brokerage:** Royalty Real Estate Boutique (Orlando, FL)

## Clients (CRM), set up like Follow Up Boss
- **Three lists:** Clients · Probates · Expireds (one click moves a probate or expired to Clients, with notes)
- **Client stages:** Lead · Hot Prospect · Nurture · Active Client · Pending · Closed · Past Client · Sphere · Trash
- **Probate & Expired stages:** New · Attempted · Talked To · Appointment Set · Not Interested · Became a Client
- **Follow-ups:** one tap: 1 day · 3 days · 1 week · 2 weeks · 1 month · pick a date. Each has a type (Probate, Expired, Client, Listing) so you can see all of one kind at once. No automatic check-in reminders.
- **Fast after-call screen:** name, phone, note, next follow-up, all in one place
- **Card:** Buyer/Seller/Both · Signed/Verbal/Not yet · stage · lead source · phones · emails · address · tags · timeframe to sell · price in mind · reason for selling · where they're moving · mortgage balance or free and clear · pre-approved, lender, price range · closing date · best time to call · preferred contact · social media · birthday · home anniversary · kids · notes timeline · follow-ups · appointments · emails sent · files · homes sent · referral info
- **Couples:** one card per couple by default
- **Day-one tags:** Sphere / Friends & Family · Investor · Executor · Vacant Property · Needs Repairs / As-Is · Out of State Owner · Referral Source · Vendor (+ Follow Up Boss tags)
- **Lead sources:** Probate · Expired listing · Sign call · Referral · Sphere · Past client · Open house · Zillow / Realtor.com · My website or social media · Royalty Real Estate Boutique
- **Smart lists (with "Start calling"):** Today's follow-ups · Overdue · Probate follow-ups due today · Expired follow-ups due today · Appointments set · Sphere & past clients not contacted in 6 months
- **Pipeline boards:** Sellers: Appointment Set → Signed → Coming Soon → Active → Pending → Closed. Buyers: Appointment Set → Signed → Showing Homes → Offer Made → Pending → Closed
- **Follow-up plans:** New probate · New expired · After listing appointment · Under contract · After closing
- **Email:** templates, "Send later," Claude drafts from your notes
- **Moving in:** export from Follow Up Boss (test a few first). Keep Follow Up Boss until the hub is checked.

## Connections
